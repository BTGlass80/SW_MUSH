# List

`list` shows what the vendor in your current room sells, with prices — the same
stock you can `buy`. If a merchant is present (for example the outfitter in a
graduate hub), `list` shows their common goods (blasters, comlinks, medpacs and
the like) so you can spend your credits.

If there's no vendor here, `list` says so and points you at `browse` for
player-run shops.

# Situation

`situation` prints the active living-world events — the same feed the SITUATION
panel shows in the web client. These are the ongoing crises, cult stirrings, and
opportunities moving through the sector on their own, whether or not you act on
them.

If nothing is stirring, `situation` says the sector is quiet. Check it to find
something happening in the wider galaxy to go get involved in.
